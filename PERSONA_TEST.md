# FirstProof — Persona Test

## Synthetic Persona
Name: Diego
Age: 22
Location: Estado de México

Diego has informal inventory experience but
does not have formal proof of his skills.

This persona is simulated.

## User Journey
1. Open FirstProof.
2. Review the inventory exercise.
3. Enter three inventory differences.
4. Declare whether AI was used.
5. Submit the exercise.
6. Review the evidence card and AI explanation.

## Observed Technical Test
Inputs:
- A101: 20
- B205: 21
- C310: 9

Result: 0/3 correct.

Expected differences:
- A101: -2
- B205: 3
- C310: 0

The AI successfully generated feedback.

## Potential Usability Issue
Users may confuse physical inventory counts
with inventory differences.

## Proposed Improvement
Add an example explaining:

System: 20
Physical count: 18
Difference: -2
